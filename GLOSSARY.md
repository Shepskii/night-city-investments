# Night City Investments

Player-run businesses, stakes and a stock market for Cyberpunk RED, played out over the game's weeks and months.

## NET runs

**NET run**:
A Netrunner's attempt to dig up evidence against a target (an NPC's business or a listed corporation), at most one a week.
_Avoid_: Hack, netrun (for NCI's use)

**Quick run**:
A NET run settled by a single Interface roll against the target's DV, for a fee, with no NET module involved.
_Avoid_: Simple run, roll run

**Real run**:
A NET run played out on a NET module's architecture that the GM has linked to the target; free, but it uses the week's NET run.
_Avoid_: Live run, full run

**NET module**:
The module that plays a real run: NET Architect, or its fork NET Architect (NCI Edition), whichever is active.
_Avoid_: NET Architecture (a different module, not used for real runs)

**Data shard**:
The evidence a NET run brings back; a real run's shard counts for more than a quick run's.
_Avoid_: Loot, file

**Traced**:
A NET run's outcome when the target notices it, raising the runner's Heat.
_Avoid_: Caught, detected
